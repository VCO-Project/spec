# Trademark Policy

"VCO" and the VCO logo are trademarks of VCO Project.

The specification text is free (CC BY 4.0), and anyone may implement
it. This policy explains how the name may be used.

## You may

- State that your implementation is "VCO-compatible" or
  "implements the VCO specification", provided it passes the
  conformance tests in vco-project/tests.
- Use the name in articles, talks, and documentation about VCO.
- Fork the specification and say "based on VCO", with attribution.
- Name a package `vco-<something>` if it is a VCO implementation
  or tool, and does not imply official status.

## You may not

- Call your fork or implementation "VCO" as its primary name.
- Imply that your implementation is official or endorsed by
  VCO Project without written permission.
- Use the VCO logo as your product's logo.

## Conformance claims

A claim of "VCO-compatible" means: the implementation passes the
conformance tests for the spec version it claims. Claims should
name the version, e.g. "VCO 0.1 compatible".

## Questions

Open an issue at vco-project/spec.
